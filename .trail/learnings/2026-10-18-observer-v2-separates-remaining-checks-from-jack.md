## Decided
A complete hardware follow-up plan separates remaining Observer evidence, owner-reported OpenBlade behavior, maintainer research, and already-covered work. Do not equate the sole remaining Synapse telemetry report with all remaining support gaps. Version 2 adds bounded displayed instructions; they cannot execute commands or grant attachment or hardware authority. Version 1 remains unchanged.

## Tried and ruled out
A temperature-only plan omitted newly implemented controls and unresolved research. Repeating all Synapse captures or every Fn key adds no evidence when exact-model source and prior bundles already establish the contract. Use representative behavior checks and conditional skips. Indented reprojection can grow a valid near-limit plan beyond 16 KiB; preserve the validated v2 object for guaranteed reimport.

## Watch out
Manual reports are append-only user observations, never firmware readback or physical promotion. Save the baseline before Begin, freeze the plan, disallow overlap with recording, require restoration reporting, and stop after unconfirmed restoration. Research and covered entries have no owner completion controls. The Blade 18 plan needs the v2 Observer UI plus the separate PDH collector fix; public 0.2.11 cannot import it. Missing temperature must not trigger load generation. Do not change charge policy or disable unrelated mask bits to force a check.
